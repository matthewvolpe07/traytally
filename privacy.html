TrayTally

Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version

TrayTally has no account, no ads and no analytics. It does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else. It works without internet. Only the one-time unlock uses Apple's App Store, for its price and to buy or restore it.

What the app saves

Your out times, wear goal, tray plan, retainer nights and settings. They are saved only on this phone, inside the app.

Backups

TrayTally's saved data is left out of iCloud and computer backups of your phone. If you move to a new phone or delete and reinstall the app, your out times and settings do not come with it. A one-time unlock you bought comes back with Restore purchase. To keep a copy of your history, use CSV export (part of the one-time unlock).

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on this phone. They do not use a server or a push service.

Exported files

A CSV file leaves the phone only when you pick a place for it in the share sheet, such as Files, iCloud Drive or a message.

Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlock, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. TrayTally collects no payment data and never sees your card or Apple Account details. To show the price and check whether the unlock was bought, the app asks the App Store from this phone; nothing about your purchase is sent to the developer.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from this phone. You can also remove single out times inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at the privacy policy link on the app's App Store page.
