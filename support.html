TrayTally

Support

TrayTally is an iPhone app for tracking clear aligner and retainer wear time, your tray schedule and retainer nights.

Contact

Questions or problems with TrayTally: email matthewvolpe07@gmail.com.

Get the one-time unlock back

On a new phone, or after you delete and reinstall the app, open TrayTally and wait a few seconds: the unlock usually comes back by itself. If the "One-time unlock" card is still at the bottom of either tab, tap "Restore purchase" on it. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My out times did not come to my new phone

TrayTally keeps its data only on your phone, and it is left out of iCloud and computer backups. Moving to a new phone or reinstalling the app starts it empty. To keep a copy of your history, use CSV export (part of the one-time unlock).

Reminders do not arrive

Check that the reminder's switch is on. "Trays out too long" and "Switch day" are in the "Reminders" card on the Aligners tab, and "Bedtime reminder" is on the Retainer tab. Then check that notifications for TrayTally are allowed in the iPhone's Settings app, under Notifications. If they are off, the app shows a note with an "Open Settings" button. Reminders are set on the phone, so they work without internet.

Export my history

With the one-time unlock, scroll to the "History" card on the Aligners tab and tap "Export out times" or "Export daily totals". The iPhone's share sheet opens, so you can save the file to Files or iCloud Drive.

Delete my data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your phone. You can also remove today's out times inside the app: under "Today's out times", tap "Edit", then tap "Remove" twice.

Medical questions

TrayTally does not give medical advice. Use the wear goal and tray schedule your own orthodontist or dentist gave you, and ask them before changing how you wear your aligners or retainer.
